Privacy Policy

TextReader · Last updated 4 August 2026

The short version. TextReader is a local, offline-first e-book reader. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. Your library, reading position, notes and statistics never leave your device unless you explicitly export them or switch on cloud translation.

Who we are

Lyrewa, Kyiv, Ukraine. Contact: lyrewa@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

All of the following is stored in the app's private storage and is not transmitted anywhere:

  • your library — file references, covers, shelves, reading progress, bookmarks, highlighted paragraphs and notes;
  • your paper-book catalogue and its cover images;
  • reading statistics — session times and pages read;
  • app settings, including your API key if you enter one;
  • caches that make reopening a book fast.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. The app can also export everything into a single ZIP file, but only when you choose that action and pick where the file goes.

Permissions

PermissionWhy
Internet, network state Downloading offline translation models, looking up book metadata by ISBN, and cloud AI translation — all only when you use those features.

The app does not request location, contacts, microphone or access to your photo library.

When data does leave your device

1. Cloud AI translation — the one that matters

If you switch the translation engine to cloud AI and enter your own API key, the app sends the text of the pages you translate directly to the provider you selected. This is a direct connection from your device to that provider; it does not pass through our servers, and we never see the text or your key.

  • Google Gemini — terms
  • OpenAI — policies
  • Anthropic — terms
  • any OpenAI-compatible endpoint you configure yourself

Your API key is stored only on your device and is sent only to that provider. If you do not enable this feature, no book text is ever transmitted.

2. On-device translation

The offline engine (Google ML Kit) downloads language models from Google the first time you use a language pair. Translation itself then runs entirely on the device — the text of your books is not sent anywhere. Model downloads are subject to Google's terms.

3. Book metadata lookup

When you scan a barcode or search for a paper book, the app sends only the ISBN or the search words you typed to public catalogues:

  • Google Books API — privacy policy
  • Open Library — privacy policy
  • Yakaboo (Ukrainian book retailer, used as a fallback source) — yakaboo.ua
  • DuckDuckGo HTML search (fallback cover lookup) — privacy policy

No identifier of you or your device is attached to these requests. They happen only when you actively add a paper book.

4. Cover images

Cover images are fetched from Open Library's public cover server (covers.openlibrary.org) by ISBN, and cached on your device.

What we never collect

We operate no servers and receive no data from the app. There is no telemetry, no advertising SDK, no third-party tracker and no crash reporting library in the build.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

Your rights

Because all data is local, you are in direct control of it: export it from Settings → Backup, or delete it by clearing app data or uninstalling. If you are in the EEA or UK, GDPR rights (access, erasure, portability) are satisfied by these controls; there is no server-side copy for us to act on.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be reflected in the "Last updated" date and, where the change affects data transmission, announced in the app's release notes.
