What is inside

Not a template with three screens — a reader people can actually live in.

📚

Seven formats

EPUB, FB2 (including .fb2.zip), PDF, HTML, plain text, and CBZ/CBR comics. Bold, italics and embedded images are preserved.

🌐

Two translation engines

On-device via ML Kit — fully offline, 50+ languages. Or cloud AI through the user's own key: OpenAI, Gemini, Anthropic, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint.

📖

A real pagination engine

Progressive tail-first pagination: the first page appears instantly while the rest is measured in the background. Position survives font changes.

✍️

Highlights & notes

Colour-coded paragraph highlights with attached notes, bookmarks, table of contents and full-text search in one navigator sheet.

🖤

E-ink ready

Auto-detects Onyx BOOX, PocketBook, Bigme and Meebook. Monochrome theme, zero animation, edge tap zones and hardware page-turn keys.

📊

Reading statistics

Sessions, streaks, time of day, pages read and a yearly reading goal — computed locally, no server involved.

📷

Paper book catalogue

Scan an ISBN barcode with the camera and the book is added with cover, publisher and page count from public catalogues.

💾

Backup & restore

Every book file, cover, note and setting exports into a single ZIP and restores on another device.

🗣️

366 strings fully localised into Ukrainian, English, Russian, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Polish, Turkish, Japanese, Chinese and Korean.

Never published — and that is deliberate. The app has never been on Google Play or any other store. So there is no signing keystore to inherit and no way to be locked out of your own updates; no existing users to migrate or support; no store history, ratings or policy strikes attached to it; and the package ID is unclaimed, so you pick your own. You are not buying someone else's listing — you are buying a finished codebase with a clean record.

One thing to know up front. Source comments are written in Ukrainian — thorough, explaining why decisions were made, not what the line does. Translating them into English is quoted separately, or you can take the project as it stands at a lower price. I would rather you hear that from me than discover it after paying.

The build is not size-optimised, on purpose. R8 shrinking and app-bundle packaging are the last step before publishing, and they have to be verified on a real device across every feature. Doing that blind, for a store I am not submitting to, would hand you a build with hidden breakage. The configuration is clean and ready for you to do it properly.
